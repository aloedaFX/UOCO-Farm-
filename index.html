About us

WE GROW FRESHNESS. WE DELIVER QUALITY.

UOCO Agro Farm is an agricultural enterprise located in Oghara, Delta State, Nigeria, focused on tomato and habanero pepper production and supply.

We are passionate about making fresh agricultural produce accessible to households, food vendors, restaurants and businesses.

From our farm to your table, our goal is to make quality produce easier to order and access.
